# Study Notes

My personal study notes from a 90-day Cybersecurity Foundations course, written in my own words as I learned.

**📄 Read the notes:** [`90 Days Cybersecurity-Foundation-myfirsthack.pdf`](90 Days Cybersecurity-Foundation-myfirsthack.pdf) (158 pages)

The notes follow the course day by day. Each day is a short write-up of the concept, why it matters to an attacker or a defender, and the examples that made it click. The ten portfolio artifacts in this repository were built from this material.

## What's covered

**Foundations (Days 1-20):** roles in cybersecurity (SOC and GRC), the 5-step audit method, how computers work, the 7-step journey of a web request, IP addresses and DNS, passwords, phishing, data, HTTPS, cookies, Wi-Fi, mobile and cloud security, how attackers work, social engineering, and building a threat model.

**Networking (Days 21-45):** ports, TCP vs UDP, firewalls, NAT, VPNs, proxies, Wireshark and reading packets, network attacks, segmentation, investigating real traffic, IDS/IPS, SIEMs, logs, network forensics, zero trust, wireless attacks, DNS tunnelling, a day in the life of a SOC analyst, and defending a small business network.

**Linux (Days 46-61):** why Linux, navigation, permissions, reading files and logs, package managers, and bash scripting.

**Concepts, attacks and defences (Days 66-80):** the CIA triad, threats vs vulnerabilities vs risks, encryption (symmetric, asymmetric, hashing), the kill chain, MITRE ATT&CK, common vulnerabilities, malware, defence in depth, vulnerability management, patching, and triaging vulnerabilities.

**Response, AI, compliance and careers (Days 81-90):** incident response, OSINT on yourself, AI as a threat and as a defender, compliance, risk management, the job landscape, what hiring managers look for, and the course wrap-up.
